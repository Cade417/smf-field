## What stays free, and what needs an account

| | Needs an account? |
|---|---|
| Maps, basemaps, 3D terrain, drawing, import/export, printing, offline areas, GPS, snap to trails, slope analysis | No |
| Show Me Fire public data (incidents, perimeters, rasters, forecast) | No |
| Third-party layers (NIFC, SNOTEL, APRS, inReach, what3words with your own key) | No account with us (their own terms apply) |
| **Team maps, live collaboration, shared positions** (served by the Show Me Fire server) | **Yes: a team owner needs a paid plan; teammates join with a code** |

The account fees help cover the cost of running the server. There is no ad tracking, and the app
only talks to the Show Me Fire server for team features when you sign in to a team.
